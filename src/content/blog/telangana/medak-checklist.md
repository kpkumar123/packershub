## Why Medak Needs Its Own Checklist

Medak's agricultural economy and mix of older cathedral-adjacent homes with newer developments mean a generic checklist misses local specifics that matter when booking packers and movers in Medak.

## Four Weeks Before: Planning and Research

Get quotes from at least three packers and movers in Medak, and specify whether your address is in the older town centre or a newer development.

## Three Weeks Before: Decluttering and Documentation

Separate what's moving from what isn't. Gather documentation for your new address.

## Two Weeks Before: Confirming Logistics

Confirm your booking in writing with a full itemised estimate. Start self-packing non-essential items if doing partial self-packing.

## One Week Before: Utilities and Final Arrangements

Notify utility providers of your move-out date. Schedule internet and cable disconnection with a buffer day. Pack a first-night bag with essentials.

## Moving Day: What to Expect

Your crew should walk you through a signed inventory before loading begins. Keep valuables and your first-night bag with you.

## The First Week After: Settling In

Unpack by priority — kitchen, bedroom, bathroom. Register your new address with the local municipal corporation.

## The First Month: Finding Your Rhythm

Give yourself time to appreciate Medak's peaceful, agricultural character and its landmark cathedral.
