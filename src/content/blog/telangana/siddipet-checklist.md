## Three Weeks Before: Decluttering and Documentation

Separate what's moving from what isn't, including sorting through farm equipment you may no longer need. Gather documentation for your new address.

## Two Weeks Before: Confirming Logistics

Confirm your booking in writing with a full itemised estimate, including any farm equipment handling costs. Start self-packing non-essential items if doing partial self-packing.

## One Week Before: Utilities and Final Arrangements

Notify utility providers of your move-out date. Schedule internet and cable disconnection with a buffer day. If transporting a vehicle, confirm your car carrier services pickup time separately. Pack a first-night bag with essentials.

## Moving Day: What to Expect

Your crew should walk you through a signed inventory before loading begins, with farm equipment noted separately if relevant. Keep valuables and your first-night bag with you.

## The First Week After: Settling In

Unpack by priority — kitchen, bedroom, bathroom — and set up any farm equipment storage at your new address. Register your new address with the local municipal corporation, and update RTO registration if you've brought a vehicle.
