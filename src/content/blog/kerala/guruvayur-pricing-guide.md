## More on what affects your final number

**GST.** 18% GST applies to the total service value for any GST-registered mover in Guruvayur — this should already be reflected in your written quote, not added as a surprise later.

**Peak season timing.** Bookings during the April–June academic transition or shortly after Onam and Diwali typically cost more than the same job booked two to three weeks out during a quieter month.

**Insurance.** Basic transit insurance is usually included in a standard quote; declared-value coverage for high-value items costs a small additional premium and is worth it for anything genuinely irreplaceable.

## Comparing quotes fairly

If you're getting quotes from multiple Guruvayur packers and movers companies, compare like for like: does each quote include a real survey, or is it a phone-based guess? Is transit insurance standard, or an optional add-on buried in fine print? A lower number with no survey behind it is rarely the cheaper option once the truck is actually loaded.

## Office relocation and vehicle transport pricing in Guruvayur

Office relocation in Guruvayur is priced separately from household shifting, since it usually involves IT asset handling and after-hours scheduling — get this quoted on its own rather than assuming it scales linearly from a household rate. Car and bike transport is likewise priced independently, by distance, vehicle type and whether you choose open or closed-carrier transport; closed-carrier costs modestly more but offers meaningfully better protection on longer routes out of Guruvayur.

## Payment terms, explained upfront

Full payment upfront, before your goods are even loaded, isn't standard practice — expect a split between a booking confirmation amount and a balance due on delivery, agreed in writing during the quote stage rather than negotiated informally on moving day.
