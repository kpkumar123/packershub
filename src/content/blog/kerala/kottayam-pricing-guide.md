## Household volume as the foundation

Our pre-move survey --- in person or via video --- estimates the actual
cubic volume of your belongings, whether you're moving a plantation-area
home, a town-centre residence, or a lakeside property near Kumarakom.
This volume decides vehicle size, crew requirement, and packing
material, and it's the basis of any honest home relocation Kottayam
quote.

## Distance and route pricing

A local move within Kottayam is priced differently from an intercity
move on the Kottayam--Kochi or Kottayam--Alappuzha corridor, and
differently again for longer relocations toward Bangalore or Chennai.
Our intercity movers Kottayam pricing reflects real distance, fuel, and
toll costs for each specific route rather than a flat per-kilometre
guess.

## Books, documents, and archival material handling

Given Kottayam's publishing heritage, book and document handling is
included in standard household pricing at no extra charge --- we don't
itemise this separately unless the volume is unusually large or the
materials are rare/archival, in which case we discuss specialised
packing during your survey.

## Access and floor considerations

Plantation-area homes with longer driveways, town-centre residences with
tighter parking, and lakeside properties with their own access quirks
all factor into labour time and vehicle sizing. These are noted during
the survey so your written quote reflects them accurately.

Full packing-moving-unpacking costs more than loading-unloading services
Kottayam alone, where you've packed your own boxes. Furniture movers
Kottayam disassembly and reassembly charges for wardrobes, beds, and

## Trade-goods handling for rubber and spice businesses

For business relocation Kottayam clients connected to the rubber and
spice trade, trade-goods handling and documentation care form a
separate, clearly itemised part of your quote, priced by volume and
material sensitivity.

## Monsoon-season cost considerations

Given Kottayam's genuinely high rainfall, moves booked during
June--September or October--November may carry slightly higher material
and scheduling costs, particularly for lakeside and plantation-adjacent
addresses --- flagged upfront at quote stage.

## Storage, priced separately

Short-term and long-term storage Kottayam is available for misaligned
move dates, priced by duration and volume as its own line item rather
than bundled into an opaque package price.

## Vehicle transport pricing

Car carrier service Kottayam and bike transport Kottayam bookings are
priced by vehicle type, distance, and carrier type, independent of your
household goods volume.

## More on what affects your final number

**GST.** 18% GST applies to the total service value for any GST-registered mover in Kottayam — this should already be reflected in your written quote, not added as a surprise later.

**Peak season timing.** Bookings during the April–June academic transition or shortly after Onam and Diwali typically cost more than the same job booked two to three weeks out during a quieter month.

**Insurance.** Basic transit insurance is usually included in a standard quote; declared-value coverage for high-value items costs a small additional premium and is worth it for anything genuinely irreplaceable.
