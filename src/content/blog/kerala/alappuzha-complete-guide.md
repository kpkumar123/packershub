## Packing tips specific to Alappuzha

Wrap fragile items individually and pack plates vertically rather than flat-stacked, since plates handle transit shock far better standing on edge. For electronics, use snug-fitting boxes with generous cushioning so there's minimal room for internal movement — photograph cable arrangements behind your TV or router before disconnecting, so reassembly at the new Alappuzha address is quick rather than guesswork. Given Kerala's monsoon patterns, moisture-resistant packing for book boxes and cardboard cartons is worth treating as standard rather than a seasonal extra, particularly for anything stored even briefly outdoors during loading.
