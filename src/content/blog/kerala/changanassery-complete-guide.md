## Packing tips specific to Changanassery

Wrap fragile items individually and pack plates vertically rather than flat-stacked, since plates handle transit shock far better standing on edge. For electronics, use snug-fitting boxes with generous cushioning so there's minimal room for internal movement — photograph cable arrangements behind your TV or router before disconnecting, so reassembly at the new Changanassery address is quick rather than guesswork.
