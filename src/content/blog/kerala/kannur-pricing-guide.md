## More on what affects your final number

**GST.** 18% GST applies to the total service value for any GST-registered mover in Kannur — this should already be reflected in your written quote, not added as a surprise later.

**Peak season timing.** Bookings during the April–June academic transition or shortly after Onam and Diwali typically cost more than the same job booked two to three weeks out during a quieter month.

**Insurance.** Basic transit insurance is usually included in a standard quote; declared-value coverage for high-value items costs a small additional premium and is worth it for anything genuinely irreplaceable.

## Comparing quotes fairly

If you're getting quotes from multiple Kannur packers and movers companies, compare like for like: does each quote include a real survey, or is it a phone-based guess? Is transit insurance standard, or an optional add-on buried in fine print? A lower number with no survey behind it is rarely the cheaper option once the truck is actually loaded.

Office relocation in Kannur is priced separately from household shifting, since it usually involves IT asset handling and after-hours scheduling — get this quoted on its own rather than assuming it scales linearly from a household rate. Full payment upfront, before your goods are even loaded, isn't standard practice — expect a split between a booking confirmation amount and a balance due on delivery, agreed in writing during the quote stage rather than negotiated informally on moving day.
