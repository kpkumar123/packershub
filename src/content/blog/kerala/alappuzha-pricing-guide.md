## Canal and bridge access as a genuine cost factor

Unlike most Kerala cities, Alappuzha's canal network can mean a larger
vehicle simply can't reach certain addresses, requiring a smaller
secondary vehicle for the final stretch. This is flagged during the
survey and reflected transparently in your written quote --- a cost
factor specific to this city's geography rather than a generic add-on.

## Distance and route pricing

A local move within Alappuzha is priced differently from an intercity

differently again for longer relocations toward Bangalore or Chennai.
Our intercity movers Alappuzha pricing reflects actual distance, fuel,

## More on what affects your final number

**GST.** 18% GST applies to the total service value for any GST-registered mover in Alappuzha — this should already be reflected in your written quote, not added as a surprise later.

**Peak season timing.** Bookings during the April–June academic transition or shortly after Onam and Diwali typically cost more than the same job booked two to three weeks out during a quieter month.

**Insurance.** Basic transit insurance is usually included in a standard quote; declared-value coverage for high-value items costs a small additional premium and is worth it for anything genuinely irreplaceable.

## Comparing quotes fairly

If you're getting quotes from multiple Alappuzha packers and movers companies, compare like for like: does each quote include a real survey, or is it a phone-based guess? Is transit insurance standard, or an optional add-on buried in fine print? A lower number with no survey behind it is rarely the cheaper option once the truck is actually loaded.
