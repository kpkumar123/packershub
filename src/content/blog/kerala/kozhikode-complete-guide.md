## Planning around Kozhikode's weather, realistically

Kerala's two monsoon windows --- the southwest monsoon from June through
September and the shorter northeast monsoon in October--November ---
aren't a minor detail for a coastal city like Kozhikode. Roads flood,
humidity spikes, and cardboard that would hold up fine in a dry state
can go soft within hours here. Our packing and moving company approach
for Kozhikode factors monsoon timing into scheduling wherever a customer
has flexibility, and uses moisture-proof packing materials as the
default regardless of season, because a delayed shipment during heavy
rain is far more common a risk here than in inland cities.

## Packing materials and methods that actually suit Kozhikode homes

Not every carton is the same, and treating them as interchangeable is
where a lot of damage claims start. For books and documents, we use

and crockery, individually wrapped items go into partitioned boxes
